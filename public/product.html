About This Product

The LosPerris Valorant API is a tool designed for Content Creators and Streamers. It provides a simplified interface to retrieve Valorant player statistics (Rank, MMR, Last Match results) and format them into plain text commands compatible with stream bots such as Nightbot, StreamElements, and Fossabot.

Intended Use: A streamer uses this website to look up their Riot ID, configure their preferred output format, and generate a dynamic URL. This URL is then added as a "Custom Command" in their chat bot, allowing viewers to see real-time stats in the chat.

Authentication

To display private player statistics, please sign in with your Riot Account.
